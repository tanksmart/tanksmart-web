Weekly Fuel Price News

A weekly look at fuel prices across Europe, covering which countries moved the most and by how much.

Nov 25, 2026
Fuel prices across Europe fall again: petrol about 11 cents cheaper in Spain, Switzerland and Ukraine

I noticed that fuel prices across Europe went down again this week, in some countries by quite a lot. Petrol is about 11 cents cheaper in Spain (1.62 €/l), Switzerland (2.17 €/l) and Ukraine (1.66 €/l). In Latvia petrol fell about 10 cents to 1.75 €/l and in Romania about 9 cents to 1.68 €/l. Diesel is also 6 to 7 cents cheaper in Spain, Ukraine, Latvia and Romania.

Diesel fell the most in Poland, about 8 cents to 1.67 €/l, while petrol there is about 5 cents cheaper (1.33 €/l). The fuel price in France dropped about 7 cents for diesel (2.09 €/l) and 5 cents for petrol (1.94 €/l). In the UK petrol is about 7 cents cheaper (1.94 €/l) and diesel about 5 cents (2.31 €/l). Slovakia is down about 7 cents on both fuels (1.62 and 1.89 €/l), Danish diesel fell about 7 cents (2.37 €/l) and the maximum diesel price in Belgium is about 6 cents lower (2.27 €/l). In Norway the new monthly figure for October shows petrol about 15 cents lower (2.39 €/l) and diesel about 13 cents lower (2.54 €/l), partly because of the weaker krone.

Fuel prices in Slovenia went down for the third week in a row: diesel is about 6 cents cheaper (1.74 €/l) and petrol about 3 cents (1.41 €/l). Diesel also fell about 6 cents in North Macedonia (1.44 €/l), and so did petrol in Bosnia (1.54 €/l). In Greece diesel is about 5 cents cheaper (1.98 €/l). Smaller drops of 5 to 6 cents were seen in San Marino, Estonia and Moldova. Finland went the other way: petrol is about 6 cents more expensive (2.20 €/l) and diesel about 8 cents (2.56 €/l). Current prices for all 42 countries are on the main comparison page.
