Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

TankSmart is a small site I run on my own. You don't need an account to use it, and I don't sell anyone's data. Below is a plain list of what the site saves, what it sends to other services, and why.

Who runs the site

The site is run by me, Ana Ranđelović, through my sole proprietorship: LunaVia, Ana Ranđelović, s.p., Cesta talcev 8, 1230 Domžale, Slovenia. For anything about your data, write to tank-smart@pm.me.

What stays in your browser

If you save a vehicle in the calculator, its details are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They never reach me. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

I use Google Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they open and roughly where they come from (country, device, browser). This helps me understand what is useful and what isn't. Google Analytics uses cookies, and the data is processed by Google, which may also transfer it to the United States. I don't see who you are, only totals.

Ads (Google AdSense)

The site is connected to Google AdSense so it can show ads in the future. When ads are shown, Google and its partners can use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to other sites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. How Google uses data from sites like this one is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Feedback form

If you send feedback through the form, I receive your message, the page and language it was sent from and, if you add one, a screenshot. The screenshot shows the page as it looked on your screen, including anything typed into the calculator. The form doesn't ask for your name or email. Messages are stored by Netlify, the company that hosts the site. I only use them to fix and improve the site. If you want a message deleted, write to me.

Email

If you write to tank-smart@pm.me, I get your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you. The mailbox is hosted by Proton Mail.

Other services the site loads

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps short technical logs of requests (such as your IP address) to run and protect the service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and one small script (used for the feedback screenshot) from cdnjs, run by Cloudflare. When your browser loads these files, those services see your IP address, as they would with any website.

Links to other sites

TankSmart links to other sites, such as Ko-fi, official vignette shops and the fuel price sources. Once you go there, their own privacy rules apply.

Cookies

TankSmart itself doesn't set cookies. Cookies come from Google Analytics and Google AdSense, described above. You can block or delete them in your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data I have about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. Write to tank-smart@pm.me. For data held by Google, you can also use your Google account settings. If you think I've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (ip-rs.si).

Changes

If anything here changes, for example when ads go live or I add a new feature, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
