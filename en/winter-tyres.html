Winter tyres in Europe: rules by country

Checked on 3 November 2026

If you drive abroad between November and April, the winter rules can change at every border. In some countries winter tyres are mandatory on fixed dates, in others only when there is snow, and in some they aren't required at all.

Below are the current rules for all 42 countries on TankSmart, with a link to the source for each one.

Before you go

  • Tyres with the Alpine symbol (a mountain with a snowflake, also called 3PMSF) are accepted everywhere. Plain M+S is no longer enough in Germany, France, Finland, Sweden, Estonia and Latvia.
  • In the Alps and the Balkans, carry snow chains even if you have winter tyres. Signs can make them mandatory on mountain roads.
  • Many countries require at least 3 or 4 mm of tread on winter tyres. ADAC recommends at least 4 mm anyway, because grip drops a lot below that.
  • Studded tyres are banned in most of Central and Southern Europe, but common in the Nordic and Baltic countries.
  • Rules can change. Check the linked source before a long trip.

Balkans

Albania

Some roads only

There is no fixed period. During snowfall the authorities can require snow chains on mountain roads, and winter tyres are recommended.

Source: ARRSH (Shqiptarja)

Bosnia and Herzegovina

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Nov to 1 Apr, regardless of the weather.

Accepted: winter tyres on all wheels, winter tyres on the drive wheels plus snow chains, or summer tyres with at least 4 mm of tread plus snow chains.

Source: Službeni glasnik BiH 83/20

Croatia

Some roads only

From 15 Nov to 15 Apr winter equipment is mandatory on designated winter sections, regardless of the weather. These include sections of the A1 to A6 motorways and the main roads through Gorski kotar and Lika. On other roads it is mandatory when there is snow or ice. Summer tyres count only with at least 4 mm of tread and snow chains for the drive wheels in the car.

Studded tyres: not allowed
Source: Narodne novine 121/2020

Kosovo

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 15 Nov to 15 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Source: KoSSev (Kosovo Police)

Montenegro

In winter conditions

Winter equipment is mandatory from 15 Nov to 1 Apr whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Summer tyres with snow chains for the drive wheels are also accepted. The police can order winter equipment on certain roads regardless of the weather.

Minimum tread: 4 mm
Source: CdM (Uprava policije)

North Macedonia

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 15 Nov to 15 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Instead of winter tyres, summer tyres are also accepted if you have snow chains in the car.

Minimum tread: 4 mmStudded tyres: not allowed
Source: MVR

Serbia

In winter conditions

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Nov to 1 Apr whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

It's best to carry snow chains, especially outside towns. Fit them where a sign requires it.

Minimum tread: 4 mm
Source: Paragraf.rs

Slovenia

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 15 Nov to 15 Mar, and outside that period whenever there are winter conditions.

Instead of winter tyres, summer tyres are also accepted if you have snow chains in the car.

Minimum tread: 3 mmStudded tyres: not allowed
Source: Policija

Central/East Europe

Austria

In winter conditions

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Nov to 15 Apr whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Snow chains instead of winter tyres are allowed only on roads fully or almost fully covered with snow or ice. Where a sign requires chains, you must fit them.

Minimum tread: 4 mmStudded tyres: allowed from 1 Oct to 31 May
Source: oesterreich.gv.at

Bulgaria

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 15 Nov to 1 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Winter tyres, or any tyres with at least 4 mm of tread, are accepted.

Minimum tread: 4 mm
Source: MVR

Czechia

In winter conditions

From 1 Nov to 31 Mar winter tyres are mandatory when the road has a continuous layer of snow, ice or frost, or when the weather makes this likely. They are also mandatory wherever the winter equipment sign (zimní výbava) is posted.

Minimum tread: 4 mmStudded tyres: not allowed
Source: Cebia

Hungary

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Signs can require winter tyres or snow chains at short notice. In heavy snow, chains may be checked at the border.

Studded tyres: not allowed
Source: ÖAMTC

Poland

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Snow chains are allowed only on snow and are mandatory where a sign requires them.

Studded tyres: not allowed
Source: ADAC

Romania

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Winter tyres are mandatory whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Winter tyres must be marked M+S.

Source: Capital.ro (RAR)

Slovakia

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Winter tyres are mandatory whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Minimum tread: 3 mmStudded tyres: not allowed
Source: Právne noviny

Southern Europe

Greece

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

From 1 Oct to 30 Apr the authorities can order snow chains or an equivalent (snow socks or M+S tyres) on certain roads, so it's best to carry chains in mountain areas.

Source: gocar.gr

Italy

Some roads only

There is no national rule. It is set by ordinance on individual roads, usually from 15 Nov to 15 Apr (in the Aosta Valley from 15 Oct). On the A22 Brenner motorway it applies regardless of the weather. Signs at the start of the road tell you.

Instead of winter tyres, summer tyres are also accepted if you have snow chains in the car.

Studded tyres: allowed from 15 Nov to 15 Mar
Source: ACI

Portugal

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Signs can require snow chains in mountain areas such as Serra da Estrela.

Source: EVZ

Spain

Some roads only

Only where signs require it or when the traffic authority (DGT) sets the red level on a road. Then you need winter tyres or snow chains.

Source: EVZ

Western Europe

Belgium

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Source: Touring

France

Some roads only

From 1 Nov to 31 Mar in designated mountain areas (Alps, Pyrenees, Massif Central, Jura, Vosges and Corsica), marked with signs at the start and end of the zone. You need four tyres with the Alpine symbol, or snow chains or socks in the car.

Source: Mobilité Club France

Germany

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Winter tyres are mandatory whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Since 1 Oct 2024 only tyres with the Alpine symbol count. Plain M+S is no longer enough, and this also applies to foreign cars.

Studded tyres: not allowed
Source: ADAC

Ireland

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Source: EVZ

Luxembourg

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Winter tyres are mandatory whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Studded tyres: allowed from 1 Dec to 31 Mar
Source: Visit Luxembourg

Netherlands

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Studded tyres: not allowed
Source: EVZ

Switzerland

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Snow chains are mandatory where the round blue sign requires them. Studded tyres are not allowed on motorways.

Studded tyres: allowed from 1 Nov to 30 Apr
Source: TCS

United Kingdom

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Source: EVZ

Scandinavia

Denmark

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Winter tyres are mandatory whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Since 1 July 2025 your tyres must suit the road conditions, so summer tyres on snow or ice are not allowed.

Studded tyres: allowed from 1 Nov to 15 Apr
Source: Færdselsstyrelsen

Finland

In winter conditions

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Nov to 31 Mar whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Studded tyres or tyres with the Alpine symbol count. Since December 2024 M+S alone is not enough. This also applies to foreign cars.

Minimum tread: 3 mmStudded tyres: allowed from 1 Nov to 31 Mar, and at other times in winter road conditions
Source: Traficom

Iceland

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

There are no fixed dates, but cars must be equipped for winter driving. From 1 Nov to 14 Apr tyres need at least 3 mm of tread.

Studded tyres: allowed from 1 Nov to 15 Apr
Source: Stjórnartíðindi

Norway

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Your car must have enough grip, so in snow or ice you need winter tyres or snow chains. From 1 Nov until the first Sunday after Easter Monday tyres need at least 3 mm of tread, and studded tyres are allowed in the same period or whenever road conditions require them (in Nordland, Troms and Finnmark from 16 Oct to 30 Apr). Oslo charges a fee for studded tyres.

Source: Statens vegvesen

Sweden

In winter conditions

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Dec to 31 Mar whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Studded tyres or tyres with the Alpine symbol count. Some streets in Stockholm, Gothenburg and Uppsala ban studded tyres.

Minimum tread: 3 mmStudded tyres: allowed from 1 Oct to 15 Apr, and at other times in winter road conditions
Source: Transportstyrelsen

Baltics

Estonia

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Dec to 1 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Only tyres with the Alpine symbol count.

Minimum tread: 3 mmStudded tyres: allowed from 15 Oct to 31 Mar
Source: ERR (Transpordiamet)

Latvia

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Dec to 1 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Only tyres with the Alpine symbol count.

Minimum tread: 4 mmStudded tyres: allowed from 1 Oct to 1 May
Source: LV portāls

Lithuania

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 10 Nov to 31 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Minimum tread: 3 mmStudded tyres: allowed from 1 Nov to 9 Apr
Source: EVZ

Micro-states

Andorra

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Nov to 15 May, regardless of the weather.

Winter tyres or snow chains are mandatory on the whole road network. Depending on the snow alert level, chains can become mandatory for everyone.

Studded tyres: allowed from 1 Nov to 15 May
Source: Govern d'Andorra

Malta

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Source: EVZ

Monaco

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Source: Automobile Club

San Marino

Some roads only

I couldn't find official winter rules for San Marino. The roads there run through Italy, where winter ordinances apply on many roads.

Source: AA Ireland

Eastern Europe (non-EU)

Belarus

Fixed dates

Winter equipment is mandatory from 1 Dec to 1 Mar, regardless of the weather.

Minimum tread: 4 mm
Source: neg.by

Moldova

In winter conditions

There are no fixed dates. Winter tyres are mandatory whenever there is snow, ice or slush on the road.

Source: TV8

Ukraine

No general obligation

There is no general winter tyre obligation.

Source: Vikna
