Mandatory car equipment in Europe: what to carry, by country

Checked on 5 November 2026

Before a trip abroad it's worth checking what you need in the car. Some countries only ask for a warning triangle, others want a vest for every passenger, a first-aid kit, spare bulbs or even a fire extinguisher.

One thing surprises a lot of people: in many countries the list of required equipment only applies to cars registered there, and a foreign car follows the rules of its home country. Rules about what you do on the road, like wearing a vest when you get out or driving with your lights on, apply to everyone. Below are the current rules for all 42 countries on TankSmart, with a source for each.

A kit that works almost everywhere

  • A vest for every passenger, kept in the cabin, not the boot. In many countries you must put it on before you step out.
  • A warning triangle. Romania asks for two, and so does Croatia if you tow a trailer.
  • A first-aid kit. Austria requires one in every car, foreign ones included, and so do most countries in the Balkans and Central Europe.
  • For the Balkans, add spare bulbs and a tow rope. For Romania and Bulgaria, a fire extinguisher.
  • Drive with dipped headlights by day. Many countries require it all year, Croatia from November to March.
